How To Play

3D Adventure: Use the Arrow Keys or W, A, S, D to walk around Celebration Island. You can also use the on-screen buttons.

When you see a big colourful station, walk close to it and press the INTERACT button.

Quick Access: Skip the walking and choose a level straight from the menu!

Reading Challenges:

  • Read the short passage carefully.
  • Answer 3 questions about the text.
  • Earn stars for correct answers! If you make a mistake, don't worry, you can try again.
